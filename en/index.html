Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026
Provider: Besimple
Contact: support@be-simple.jp

This policy explains what information BlobDiet (“the app”) handles, how it is used, and where it is kept.

Who we are

  • BlobDiet is made and provided by Besimple. Besimple is the trade name of a sole proprietor based in Japan. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Besimple.
  • For anything about your information, email support@be-simple.jp. This is our privacy contact. Some laws call this a privacy officer or a data protection officer. You can write to us from any country.
  • We don’t list a name, postal address or phone number here. If you ask by email, we’ll tell you without delay.

Information the app handles

  • Photos: photos you take to log meals, and photos you choose from the Photos app.
  • Food you type: the names and amounts of food you type in “Add by typing” in your log.
  • Meal logs: item names, kcal, protein / fat / carbs, and how much you ate.
  • Body details: sex, birth year and month, height, weight, and how active you usually are. If you are under 18, the app doesn’t ask for your height and weight.
  • Plan details: how many times AI scanned your photos or looked up your food today, the day your 3-day trial started, and whether you have Plus.

There is no login. We don’t collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
We don’t use ads, analytics tools, or tracking across other apps and websites.

What the information is used for

  • To keep your meal logs and show them in the app.
  • To work out your daily burn from your body details. If you don’t enter body details, the app uses 2,000 kcal for now.
  • To show estimated fat from what you logged and your daily burn.
  • To get a rough estimate of dish names and kcal with AI, only if you agree (see below).
  • To check your plan and today’s AI limit.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your meal logs, and the food stickers cut from your photos, stay only on your iPhone.
  • Your body details stay only on your iPhone.
  • When AI gives an answer in “Add by typing”, the app remembers it on your iPhone. It uses it the next time you type the same food.
  • The number of AI uses and the day your trial started are kept only on your iPhone.
  • Whether you have Plus is checked on your iPhone with Apple’s system. We don’t receive your name or payment details.
  • The app has no server. We can’t see your photos or logs.

Backups

  • Your body details, meal logs and food stickers are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer). If you move to a new iPhone and restore from that backup, you can keep using them.

Body details and health

  • Some laws may treat details like your weight, your height and what you eat as health information. We handle them with that level of care either way.
  • Body details are used only on your iPhone, to work out your daily burn. They are never sent to us, to Anthropic, or to anyone else.
  • Meal photos and food you type are sent to AI only if you agree.

When photos and typed food are sent to AI (only if you agree)

To get a rough estimate of dish names and kcal, the app sends your photos and the food you type to Anthropic. Anthropic is a US company that makes the AI Claude.

  • When we ask: The first time you take a photo, or the first time “Add by typing” needs AI, the app asks if it may send. Nothing is sent until you agree.
  • What is sent (photos): one photo, made smaller so its long side is 768 pixels, and where the food is in the photo. For receipts, order screens and nutrition labels, the text your iPhone read from the photo is sent too. Photo details such as the date, time and place (Exif) are removed.
  • What is sent (typed food): only the parts of what you typed that your iPhone couldn’t find kcal for on its own, as you typed them. Common foods are not sent. They are sent together, once, when you tap “Get kcal”. No photo is sent with them.
  • What is not sent: your body details and your meal logs. We never add anything that shows who you are, such as your name or contact details.
  • Personal details in photos and text: Before sending, the app looks for phone numbers, postcodes, card numbers, delivery addresses and similar details on receipts and order screens, and blanks them out. It may not find all of them. If a screen shows your name or address, crop that part out before you choose the photo, or take the photo so it doesn’t show. In “Add by typing”, please type only what you ate, not names or contact details.
  • Network: Because of how the internet works, your IP address reaches the receiver.
  • App check: To confirm that a real copy of this app on a real iPhone is sending, the app also sends Anthropic a proof made with Apple’s App Attest. The key behind it is made only for this app on this iPhone. It doesn’t include anything that shows who you are, such as your name, email address or Apple Account.
  • Plans and limits: The app sends to AI only while you have Plus or are in your 3-day trial. On Basic, nothing is sent, neither photos nor typed food. There is a daily limit on AI uses. You can check it on the Plan screen.
  • What it is used for: only to get dish names, kcal, and protein / fat / carbs.
  • At Anthropic: The photos and text you send, and the answers, are not used to train AI. They are stored in the US and are normally deleted within 30 days. They may be kept longer in some cases, such as a suspected violation of Anthropic’s usage rules. Processing may also happen in countries other than the US. For details, see Anthropic’s privacy center (https://privacy.claude.com).
  • Photos not sent yet: When there is no signal, for example, the photo is kept on your iPhone for a while and sent later. Once it has been scanned, that copy is deleted.

It’s safer to take photos that don’t show people’s faces.

Sending information outside your country

  • Where it goes: the United States. Processing may also happen in other countries.
  • Privacy law in the US: The US doesn’t have one national privacy law like those in Australia, New Zealand, Singapore or Japan. Instead, there are laws for certain fields and laws in some states.
  • How it is protected: We send this information under Anthropic’s commercial terms, which include data processing terms. These terms are the steps we take to make Anthropic protect your information. Under them, Anthropic uses what we send only to give us its service, doesn’t use it to train AI, and normally deletes it within 30 days (it may keep information linked to a suspected violation of its usage rules for up to 2 years).
  • In short: In the US, your information is protected mainly by Anthropic’s promises in these terms, not by one national privacy law.
  • If you live in Japan: Japan’s Personal Information Protection Commission has a report on US privacy laws (in Japanese): https://www.ppc.go.jp/enforcement/infoprovision/laws/offshore_report_america/
  • If you don’t want your information to go to the US, don’t agree, or turn sending off later (see below).

If you don’t agree, or want to stop

  • If you don’t agree, you can’t log by photo. The app asks again when you tap the camera button. You can still log meals by typing: open your log at the bottom right and tap “Add food”. Common foods get kcal without AI. If kcal can’t be found, a rough kcal is logged, so please fix it yourself later.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time. Turn off “Photo scanning” in the app’s Settings, or go to iPhone Settings → BlobDiet → “Scan photos with AI”. The same switch also stops sending the food you type.
  • Once you turn it off, nothing more is sent. Photos waiting to be sent are not sent either.

Seeing and correcting your information

  • Body details: You can see and change them in the app. Tap the person button at the top left of the home screen.
  • Meal logs: You can see, edit and remove items in your log. Open it at the bottom right of the home screen.
  • We don’t hold your photos, meal logs or body details, and we don’t keep copies of what is sent to Anthropic. So we have nothing of yours to show you or correct.
  • You can still ask us about your information, or about how it is handled, at support@be-simple.jp. We’ll reply within 30 days. There is no charge.

Deleting your information

  • Meal logs and photos: tap “Erase all logs” in the app.
  • Body details: tap “Erase body details” in the app.
  • Everything: delete the app from your iPhone. Everything the app kept on your iPhone is deleted. Copies in your backups are deleted when you delete those backups.

We don’t have your photos or logs, so there is nothing for us to delete.
Photos and text sent to Anthropic are normally deleted by Anthropic within 30 days, as described above. We can’t ask Anthropic to delete them sooner, because Anthropic doesn’t accept such requests.

Questions and complaints

  • If you have a question or a complaint about how we handle your information, email support@be-simple.jp and tell us what happened. We’ll look into it and reply within 30 days.
  • If you’re not happy with our reply, you can contact the privacy regulator where you live:
    • Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (https://www.oaic.gov.au)
    • New Zealand: Office of the Privacy Commissioner (https://www.privacy.org.nz)
    • Singapore: Personal Data Protection Commission (https://www.pdpc.gov.sg)
    • Japan: Personal Information Protection Commission (https://www.ppc.go.jp/en/)

  • We don’t hold your photos, meal logs or body details. The app has no server.
